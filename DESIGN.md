## Overview

A small desktop window for opening vaults and setting launch at login. Light mode is a warm off-white ground with paler cards; dark mode is near-black with darker cards. System type, 1px hairlines, almost no shadow. One violet marks the primary action; everything else is neutral.

## Colors

| Role                                                                  | Light     | Dark         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- | ------------ |
| `background`                                                          | `#faf5e8` | `#161618`    |
| `card`, `popover`, `sidebar`, `paper`                                 | `#fffaf0` | `#0c0c0e`    |
| `muted`, `accent`, `secondary`                                        | `#f5f0e0` | `#161618`    |
| `sidebar-preview`                                                     | `#ebe6d6` | `#1d1d20`    |
| `foreground`, `ring`, `link`, and the `-foreground` roles on surfaces | `#0a0a0a` | `#ffffff`    |
| `muted-foreground`                                                    | `#6a6a6a` | white at 60% |
| `paper-foreground`                                                    | `#3a3a3a` | white at 85% |
| `border`, `input`, `sidebar-border`                                   | `#e5e5e5` | white at 12% |
| `primary`                                                             | `#534fb2` | `#a8a5ff`    |
| `primary-foreground`                                                  | `#ffffff` | `#0a0a0a`    |
| `primary-active`                                                      | `#3d3495` | `#8d88eb`    |
| `sidebar-primary`                                                     | `#0a0a0a` | `#0a0a0a`    |

- Status colors are the same in both modes: destructive `#ef4444`, success `#22c55e`, warning `#f59e0b`.
- `brand-primary` is ochre `#e8b94a`. The chart roles are pink, teal, lavender, peach, and ochre.
- The `brand-light-*` and `brand-dark-*` primitives are two violet scales. `brand-light-500` is the logo color on light grounds and `brand-dark-300` on dark grounds.

## Typography

- Interface: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`. Paths and code: `source-code-pro, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, 'Courier New', monospace`.
- Five steps, size over leading: meta 12/16px, dense 13/18px, control 14/20px, shell title 15/20px, body 16/24px.
- Text is antialiased. The shell turns off synthesized bold and italic.

## Layout

- The window is fixed at 800 × 650.
- The shell is a 40px app bar over a body. Sidebars run 220 to 460px (280px by default), the auxiliary pane 220px or more (310px by default), and the main content keeps at least 150px.
- Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, and 96px. Cards pad 24px. A document page is at most 720px wide; content is at most 1280px.

## Elevation & Depth

- Surfaces separate by tone and hairlines.
- Overlays take one shadow: `0 18px 44px` black at 16%.
- Stacking order: base 0, divider 2, auxiliary 20, content 30, docked sidebar 40, chrome 50, overlay 60, modal 80.

## Shapes

- Tailwind's `rounded-sm` to `rounded-xl` are 6.4, 8.4, 10.4, and 14.4px; the base radius is 10.4px.
- Fixed radii: 6, 8, 12, 16, and 24px, plus pills. A paper sheet has a 16px radius.
- Controls use 3, 6, 7, and 9px radii.

## Components

- Core: shadcn components on Base UI (`base-vega`), vendored unchanged. Elements adjust core; patterns build the shell, the vault switcher, the vault list and rows, the create form, and the engine-unreachable notice.
- Controls are 18, 24, 28, or 36px tall. Icons are 14 or 16px on a 24 viewbox, stroke drawn at 0.85.
- The app bar is 40px tall, with 27px compact controls, a 16px rail inset, and a 15px title.
- Motion: 80 to 200ms. Panels move on a spring curve (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`) and close on `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)`; fades use `ease-out`.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do take every color, size, radius, and duration from the generated custom properties.
- Do add a value to `components/design/visual-tokens.json` and regenerate; don't write it into a stylesheet.
- Don't edit vendored core components; enrich them in elements.

## Responsive Behavior

- The window does not resize. Each sidebar is closed, previewed over the content, or docked beside the content. A sidebar resizes between its minimum and maximum width.
- With reduced motion, shell transitions take 0.01ms.

## Known Gaps

- Dark mode applies once the theme provider sets `.dark` on the root; until the window's script runs, the page is light.
- The tokens set no `color-scheme`, so native controls and scrollbars stay light in dark mode.
- No style uses the `brand-light-*` and `brand-dark-*` scales or the primitives `primary-active`, `body-strong`, `muted-soft`, `hairline-soft`, `surface-dark`, `surface-dark-elevated`, `on-dark`, `on-dark-soft`, `brand-mint`, and `brand-coral`.
