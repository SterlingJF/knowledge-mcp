## What is knowledge-mcp?

knowledge-mcp helps your AI assistant revise documents, work through new notes and organize files in your knowledge base. It gives your assistant instructions for each kind of document, plus context, change plans and edit history.

Your assistant still does the reading, drafting and editing with its own tools. knowledge-mcp isn't an assistant, and it doesn't store your files. In a codebase, it helps with documentation and records, not with running code.

![An AI chat reviews a design proposal with knowledge-mcp, records the approved edit, then picks it up in a new conversation](/images/knowledge-mcp-demo.gif)

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get oriented">

    Ask your assistant what this folder is about and [where to start](/quick-start#start-or-resume-work). It answers from the definitions already in your knowledge base.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start work">

    Ask it to revise a document or help you with your notes.

    ![The assistant reads the proposal and evidence while knowledge-mcp returns the document type, reader and checks](/images/km-still-2-trace.png)

  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer questions">

    Your assistant checks the instructions that apply and asks you about anything that's unclear.

    ![Proposed changes table with sources, a left-out vendor quote and two questions for the user](/images/km-still-1-proposal.png)

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review changes">

    Check the new text, its sources and which file it goes in. Your assistant waits for your approval before saving it.

    ![Revised proposed changes with the user's decisions recorded as the source and nothing saved yet](/images/km-still-3-decided.png)

  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue later">

    Your assistant saves document settings and records each edit, so you can come back to this knowledge base later and carry on.

    ![A new conversation recalls the approved revision and what is still open](/images/km-still-4-next-session.png)

  </Step>
</Steps>
